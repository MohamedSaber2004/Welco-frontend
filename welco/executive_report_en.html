2. Orders Management & PDF Export Engine

Administrative Requirement: Provide PDF export options for individual orders, a consolidated master list of all orders, and official commercial tax invoices.

2.1 Individual Order Consignment & Verification Sheet (Single Order PDF)

  • Purpose & Utility: Allows administrators, inventory supervisors, and dispatch staff to generate a formal, printable PDF for any individual order. This document is utilized for warehouse picking, packing inspection, quality checks, and as a formal consignment receipt for hospitals and purchasing clinics.
  • Data Included in the Document: Unique Order Reference Number, submission date, comprehensive buyer details (hospital name, clinic address, contact person), an itemized product table including official SKU codes, product names, shipped quantities, unit prices, subtotal breakdowns, applied discounts, shipping costs, and official Welco branding with ISO quality credentials.
  • Location & How to Use:
    1. From the left navigation menu of the Admin Dashboard, click "Orders".
    2. Click on any order row to open its details modal, or click the action menu icon next to the order.
    3. Click the button labeled "Export Order (PDF)".
    4. The PDF downloads immediately to your computer, fully formatted for standard A4 printing.

2.2 Master Orders Registry & Consolidated Report (All Orders PDF)

  • Purpose & Utility: Enables management to extract an executive, horizontal (Landscape) overview of all orders across the platform for administrative reviews, audit logs, and accounting reconciliations.
  • Data Included in the Report: A structured table summarizing every order: Order ID, order date, medical facility name, total item count, gross value in USD, and current processing status (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered), along with an executive header summary detailing total order counts and aggregate sales volume.
  • Location & How to Use:
    1. In the "Orders" section, navigate to the top toolbar header.
    2. Click the button labeled "Export Orders Report (PDF)".
    3. If you have applied any filters (such as orders within a specific date range or only completed orders), the generated PDF automatically respects your exact filter criteria.

2.3 Official Commercial Tax Invoice (Commercial Invoice PDF)

  • Purpose & Utility: Generates a compliant, formal tax invoice meeting standard hospital procurement and institutional accounting requirements, suitable for financial settlements and customs clearance.
  • Data Included in the Invoice:
    • Official sequential invoice reference number (e.g., INV-XXXX).
    • Registered seller identification (Welco Manufacturing) with commercial tax and ISO registrations.
    • Purchasing hospital or healthcare institution credentials.
    • Itemized billing schedule detailing catalog items, unit rates, applicable tax percentages, and full currency figures.
    • Dedicated footer space for official company stamping, signature, and executive sign-off.
  • Location & How to Use: Available directly in the orders table via the invoice receipt icon, or within the order details window by clicking "Tax Invoice (PDF)".

3. Healthcare Companies & Providers Directory (Companies PDF)

Administrative Requirement: Ability to export a structured list of all registered healthcare institutions, providers, and suppliers in PDF format.

3.1 Registered Companies Directory Report (Companies Registry PDF)

  • Purpose & Utility: Equips management with a consolidated printable directory of all healthcare clients, specialized surgical centers, distributors, and certified suppliers registered on the Welco platform.
  • Data Included in the Document: Sequential company index, legal business name, clinical entity classification (Hospital, Specialized Clinic, Equipment Distributor, Medical Supplier), geographic jurisdiction and country, verified email address, official telephone number, and compliance verification status (Verified & Approved, Under Review, or Suspended).
  • Location & How to Use:
    1. From the left navigation menu, open "Companies".
    2. In the top toolbar, click the button labeled "Export Companies (PDF)".
    3. The platform instantly compiles all active records into a clear, print-ready Landscape PDF document.

4. Product Management & New Product Controls (Product / Media)

Administrative Requirement:
1) Make Price, Stock Quantity, and Arabic Product Name optional fields when creating a new product.
2) Enforce a minimum requirement of at least 3 product images, with a dedicated field for a product demonstration video.

4.1 Flexible Field Controls (Optional Fields)

  • Price Field: Price is no longer mandatory. Products can be created and published without setting a fixed price.
    Operational Benefit: Essential for high-precision surgical instruments, customized surgical sets, or items sold through custom Requests for Quote (RFQ) or volume-based pricing. If a price is entered, it is saved and displayed accurately.
  • Stock Quantity Field: Stock quantity is completely optional. Leaving this field blank will not block product creation or publishing.
    Operational Benefit: Accommodates made-to-order instruments, custom surgical implants, and catalog items supplied continuously by manufacturers without maintaining local fixed inventory counts.
  • Arabic Product Name: The Arabic name is optional. Adding only the standard international English nomenclature is fully accepted.
    Operational Benefit: Expedites bulk catalog onboarding of specialized surgical tools with standard international names without requiring immediate translation, while retaining full support for Arabic text whenever needed.
  • Visual Labeling: All three fields are clearly marked with an "(Optional)" label on the admin screen to ensure clarity for data entry staff.

4.2 Media Quality Assurance (Images & Video URL)

  • Mandatory Minimum of 3 Images: To maintain hospital-grade catalog standards, the system enforces uploading at least 3 distinct images per product. This guarantees that surgeons and procurement officers can examine instrument tips, gripping handles, and craftsmanship from multiple angles.
  • Interactive Media Counter: The admin interface features a smart visual indicator showing remaining images required (e.g., "1 of 3 required images uploaded"). The counter turns to an approved checkmark once 3 images are provided, with additional slots easily added via a dedicated button.
  • Product Demonstration Video URL: A dedicated field is provided for product video links (such as YouTube or direct video streams demonstrating tool functionality or sterilization guidelines). The video player embeds seamlessly on the public product page for prospective buyers.
  • Location & How to Use:
    1. From the left navigation menu, navigate to "Products" and click "Add New Product".
    2. Notice the optional indicators beside Price, Stock, and Arabic Name, alongside the 3 pre-configured image upload slots and the Video URL field.

5. Direct Contact Channels (Contact Us Integration)

Administrative Requirement: Integrate direct communication channels (Email, Phone, Facebook, Instagram) with full management control from the Admin Dashboard, displaying interactively on the public website.

5.1 Active Channels & Customer Experience

  • 1. Direct Email (Mail / Email):
    • Admin Control: Dedicated field to update the official customer care and inquiries email.
    • Customer Experience: Appears in the site-wide footer and support pages; clicking the email immediately opens the client's mail client with a pre-addressed message to Welco.
  • 2. Direct Telephone / Contact (Phone / Contact):
    • Admin Control: Dedicated field to record the primary phone number with country dialing code.
    • Customer Experience: Operates as an instant click-to-call link; mobile users who tap the number initiate an immediate telephone call without manual dialing.
  • 3. Official Facebook Page:
    • Admin Control: Input field to maintain the official Welco Facebook URL.
    • Customer Experience: An interactive Facebook badge appears in the footer and help center, launching Welco's official page in a new secure window.
  • 4. Official Instagram Profile:
    • Admin Control: Input field to maintain the official Welco Instagram account URL.
    • Customer Experience: An interactive Instagram badge connects buyers to Welco's photo catalog and exhibition showcase.

5.2 Real-Time Autonomous Synchronization

  • No Developer Intervention Needed: Administrators can update any phone number, email address, or social link directly from the dashboard settings at any time.
  • Immediate Live Update: Clicking "Save" immediately broadcasts changes across all public pages, headers, footers, and support sections without waiting for IT updates or platform redeployment.
